Common Questions常见问题

Answers before you pick up the phone.在您打电话之前的答案。

How much will my job cost?我的工程大概要多少钱？
Every job is different, so we don't use price lists. After a free on-site visit, you get a fixed-price, itemised written quote, with no vague allowances and no surprises later.每个项目都不同，所以我们不使用价格表。免费现场勘查后，您会收到固定价格的明细书面报价，没有模糊预留，后期无意外。
Do I need council approval for a deck?露台需要议会审批吗？
Often not. In NSW many small decks count as exempt development. We check your site and tell you which applies before we quote.通常不需要。在 NSW，许多小型露台属于豁免开发。我们会检查您的场地并在报价前告知您适用哪种情况。
How much deposit do you ask for?你们收取多少定金？
Never more than 10% of the contract price, which is the NSW limit. The rest is paid in stages set out in your contract as the work progresses.不超过合同价格的 10%，这是 NSW 的法定上限。其余款项按合同规定的阶段支付。
Will I get a written contract?会有书面合同吗？
Yes. NSW law requires a written contract for any job over $5,000. Jobs over $20,000 also need home warranty insurance (HBCF) in place before any deposit is taken.会的。NSW 法律规定任何超过 5000 澳元的工程都必须有书面合同。超过 2 万澳元的工程还需要在收取任何定金前办理房屋保修保险（HBCF）。
Are you licensed and insured?你们持牌并投保了吗？
Yes. Ziverse Constructions works under NSW contractor licence 471023C and carries $5 million public and products liability insurance.是的。Ziverse Constructions 持有 NSW 承包商执照 471023C，并投保了 500 万澳元的公共及产品责任险。
Is the work guaranteed?工程有保修吗？
All residential building work in NSW is covered by statutory warranties: 6 years for major defects and 2 years for other defects.NSW 所有住宅建筑工程都受法定保修保障：主要缺陷 6 年，其他缺陷 2 年。
How long will it take?工程需要多长时间？
It depends on the size of the job and any approvals. Your quote includes a start date and an expected timeframe, and you get regular progress updates.取决于项目规模和审批情况。您的报价会包含开始日期和预计工期，施工过程中会有定期进度更新。
Which areas do you cover?你们服务哪些区域？
All of Greater Sydney and the Blue Mountains, including the Hills District, Western Sydney, Parramatta, Inner West, North Shore, Northern Beaches, Eastern Suburbs, St George & Sutherland Shire and South West Sydney.大悉尼地区及蓝山，包括 Hills District、Western Sydney、Parramatta、Inner West、North Shore、Northern Beaches、Eastern Suburbs、St George & Sutherland Shire 和 South West Sydney。
